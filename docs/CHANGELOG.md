| 0.86.0 | 2026-11-19 | Curves, part 4: making curves from curves. Two-View Curve: two flat curves drawn in two views (side and top, or side and front) become one 3D curve along the axis both run along - the classic way to draw character lines over blueprints. Curve Fillet: round corner points with a radius, cut back exactly along the curve on both sides and joined G1 (edit mode: selected corners; object mode: every corner of the selected curves). Offset Curve: a parallel copy of a flat curve in its plane, corners joined round. Extend Curve: lengthen ends - Natural continues the end segment's own cubic (perfectly smooth), Straight goes on along the tangent. A point added at an end of a curve takes that end's tilt and radius. Core: core/curvemake.py. This completes the curve editing roadmap (A-F) |
| 0.85.0 | 2026-11-19 | Curves, part 3. Attach Curve Ends: an end goes to the nearest thing in reach - another curve's end (G0 joins it, G1 continues its tangent, G2 its bend too; the next point is kept smooth), a patch (onto the surface; G1 puts the end tangent in the tangent plane, or Off the Surface leaves along the normal), or another curve (a T junction). In Curve Points, dragging an end point moves it and snaps it onto patch edges and other curves (S toggles). Point Continuity colours every curve point G2 green / G1 yellow / corner red / end grey and follows edits, in edit mode too; the curvature comb refreshes every 0.15 s (was 0.4) so it follows a drag. Both are also in the Curves panel. Core: core/curveends.py |
| 0.84.0 | 2026-11-19 | Curves, part 2. Constrained Move (edit mode, modal): the selected points move along their own normal (towards the bend), tangent or binormal, or only their handle lengths change (H); K keeps G2 at the points that had it. Make G2: equal curvature on both sides of a point by its handle lengths alone (positions and directions kept); all points of a curve are solved together (Levenberg-Marquardt on the log lengths: point by point, the passes drifted into inflections); corners, inflections and points by a straight side are left and reported. Refit Curve: the same shape with the fewest points within a tolerance (% of the size) or a given count, corners and ends kept. Core: core/curvesmooth.py |
| 0.83.0 | 2026-11-19 | Curves panel, first part of the curve editing roadmap. Curve Points (object or edit mode, modal): click on a curve to add a point (exact, the shape does not change); drag on a curve to pull it (Curve Pull: the grabbed point follows the mouse exactly, the rest follows by a falloff measured along the curve; Wheel range, F falloff, E pinned ends with their tangents, N crossing curves of a network follow so the network stays joined, V/P/X/Y/Z view plane / the curve's own plane / an axis; smooth points stay smooth); drag a point to slide it along the curve, Ctrl+click to remove one (the curve is refitted and its neighbours keep their tangents); Ctrl+Z, Enter, Esc as in Soft Pull. In edit mode, Slide Points slides the selected points along the curve (like Vertex Slide) and Dissolve Points removes them keeping the shape; both are in the right-click menu. Core: core/curvepoint.py (exact insert, a span fit with fixed end tangents solved by Levenberg-Marquardt), core/curvepull.py |
